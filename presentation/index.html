  • No savings buffer

  • Language barriers
  • Distrust of banks
  • Scam targeting

🏦 Structural

  • KYC barriers
  • No credit record
  • Minimum balances

🧠 Behavioural

  • Financial stress
  • Short planning horizon
  • Shame/avoidance
