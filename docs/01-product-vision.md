# 01 — Product Vision

## Mission

To give every person with an irregular income the tools, knowledge, and protection to manage money with confidence — regardless of literacy, language, connectivity, or access to formal banking.

## Vision

A world where financial confidence is not a privilege of the formally employed. MoneyMate becomes the trusted financial companion in the pocket of the informal economy — proactively guiding people through the ups and downs of irregular income, building savings habits that stick, teaching money skills in the moment they're needed, and standing between users and the scams that prey on the financially vulnerable.

By 2030, MoneyMate aims to serve 20 million underserved users across emerging markets, measurably lifting their savings rates, financial literacy, and resilience to financial shocks.

## Problem Statement

> **How might we make everyday money management simpler, safer, and more useful for people with irregular incomes, limited financial knowledge, or limited access to formal financial services?**

People in the informal and gig economy face a compounding set of challenges that mainstream fintech ignores:

- **Irregular income** makes traditional monthly budgeting models useless. Income arrives in unpredictable bursts.
- **Limited financial literacy** means generic advice ("save 20% of your salary") doesn't translate to action.
- **No savings discipline infrastructure** — no automatic payroll deductions, no employer pension.
- **High scam exposure** — these users are disproportionately targeted by fraud (fake loans, phishing, pyramid schemes) and have the least margin to absorb a loss.
- **Language barriers** — most financial apps are English-only.
- **Expensive mobile data & low connectivity** — heavy, always-online apps are unusable or unaffordable.

Existing banking and fintech apps assume a stable salary, financial fluency, a good data connection, and English. MoneyMate assumes none of these.

## Strategic pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Signature feature |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Simpler** | Money management adapted to irregular income, in plain language and the user's own tongue | AI Budget Planner, Voice Assistant, Multilingual Engine |
| **Safer** | Active protection against fraud and risky decisions | Scam Shield, explainable AI, transparent data use |
| **More useful** | Builds durable habits and skills, not just dashboards | Micro-Savings Engine, Financial Literacy Coach, Goal Setting |
| **Reachable** | Works on cheap phones, slow networks, and offline | Offline Mode, USSD/SMS fallback, low-data design |

## Success Metrics

Metrics are grouped by the North Star and supporting dimensions. Each has a definition and a target for Year 1 post-launch.

### North Star Metric

**Financial Confidence Score (FCS) uplift** — a composite index (0–100) per active user combining: savings consistency, emergency-fund coverage, literacy module completion, and self-reported confidence (quarterly in-app survey).
- **Target:** +15 points average FCS uplift within 6 months of activation.

### Adoption & inclusion

| Metric | Definition | Y1 Target |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Monthly Active Users (MAU) | Users with ≥1 meaningful action/month | 500,000 |
| Underserved reach | % of users previously unbanked/underbanked | ≥ 60% |
| Feature-phone reach | % of active users via USSD/SMS | ≥ 20% |
| Language coverage | Non-English sessions as % of total | ≥ 50% |
| Offline usage | % of sessions started offline | ≥ 30% |

### Engagement & habit

| Metric | Definition | Y1 Target |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| D30 retention | Active on day 30 after signup | ≥ 40% |
| Savings streak | Users with ≥4 consecutive weekly micro-saves | ≥ 35% of savers |
| Budget adherence | Users staying within AI budget envelopes | ≥ 50% |
| Literacy completion | Users completing ≥3 coach modules | ≥ 45% |

### Impact (the reason we exist)

| Metric | Definition | Y1 Target |
|--------|------------|-----------|

| Emergency-fund coverage | Users with ≥1 month expenses buffered | +20 pp |
